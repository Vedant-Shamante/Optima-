Risk Mitigation Logic
How the Digital Twin can respond when risk increases.
When predicted mechanical risk increases, the optimizer can evaluate a lower SRP speed or modified stroke length. If thermal risk increases, the system can evaluate steam volume, pressure and soak-time adjustments. The final operating recommendation is generated by considering production, energy and mechanical constraints together.
